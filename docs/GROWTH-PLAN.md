# LiveLifeTricks Growth Plan: from calculators to a ₹15 lakh sale

## The target

Content and tool websites usually sell for **30–40 times their average monthly profit** over the last 12 months.

| Sale price | Monthly profit needed, kept up for 12+ months |
|---|---|
| **₹15,00,000** | **about ₹40,000–50,000** |

At Indian finance-niche earnings (ads at about ₹150–300 per 1,000 page views, plus affiliate sign-ups), that means roughly **1.5–2.5 lakh page views a month**. A realistic timeline is **18–30 months**.

## Why calculators

- **People return to them.** Tax and EMI calculators get used every year at budget time, at tax-filing time and whenever someone is shopping for a loan.
- **Google can't fully answer them.** It can summarise an article in its AI answers, but people still click through to put their own numbers into a calculator.
- **Buyers like them.** Tools earn steadily, need little upkeep and don't depend on a writer.
- **Finance pays more.** Ads on tax and loan pages pay several times more than general tips content.

## Phase 3 – Guides that feed the calculators (Months 2–12)

Publish **3–4 well-researched guides a week**, each linking to a calculator. Examples:

- "Old vs new tax regime for ₹10 / 15 / 20 / 25 lakh salary": a series of pages for each salary level, each with its numbers worked out
- "How to save tax on ₹12 lakh salary", "Is ₹12.75 lakh salary tax-free?"
- "Home loan EMI for ₹30 / 40 / 50 lakh": a page for each loan amount with a full table
- "How to claim HRA without rent receipts", "80C full list"
- A Budget-day update every 1 February. Being among the first sites updated brings a big traffic spike.

**Rules:** every page is checked by a person, gives real numbers and shows its "Last updated" date. Never publish mass AI-generated pages. Google penalises them, and buyers check for them before they pay.

## Phase 4 – Traffic beyond Google (Months 2–24)

- **YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels:** 30-second clips such as "₹15 lakh salary: old vs new regime", with the calculator link
- **WhatsApp channel and Telegram:** tax deadline reminders and budget updates
- **Email list:** offer a free "Tax saving checklist" PDF. A list makes the site worth more to buyers.
- **Links:** answer questions on Quora and Reddit with calculator links, write guest posts on finance blogs, and pitch data stories to journalists (for example, "the city where people save the most tax with HRA")

## Risks

- **Google updates** can cut traffic quickly. To soften this, build traffic from other places too (email, YouTube, WhatsApp) and keep content quality high.
- **Wrong tax figures damage trust.** Update the tax engine after every Budget and keep the tests passing.
- **Competition** (ClearTax, Groww and banks): win by being faster, simpler and more specific, with salary-level pages, plain answers and "which is better" verdicts.
