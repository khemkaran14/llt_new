About LiveLifeTricks

We make money decisions simpler for everyday people in India.

Tax rules, loan offers and investment options change every year, and most explanations are full of jargon. LiveLifeTricks turns them into free calculators and short, plain-language guides, so you can see the answer to your own situation in seconds.

How we keep our calculators accurate

  • We follow the Finance Act, Income Tax Department notices and RBI updates, and update the calculators when rules change.
  • Every calculator is checked against worked examples before it goes live.
  • Each page shows the date it was last updated.

Your privacy

All calculations run in your own browser. We don't ask for your name, PAN or phone number, and the figures you type are never sent to us.
