    How "Add to calendar" works

    • Google Calendar: opens Google Calendar with the deadline filled in. Tap Save.
    • Apple / Outlook: downloads a calendar file. Open it and your phone adds the deadline, with an alert 3 days before.
    • Add all: adds every upcoming deadline for your filter in one go.

    Who has to pay advance tax?

    If your tax for the year, after TDS, is more than ₹10,000, you must pay it in four instalments instead of all at once while filing. This usually affects freelancers, business owners, landlords and people with large capital gains or interest income. Salaried people whose employer deducts TDS usually don't need to. Senior citizens without business income are exempt.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the last date to file ITR for FY 2026-27?

    31 July 2027 for salaried people and others who don't need an audit (ITR-1 and ITR-2). Business and professional taxpayers who don't need an audit have until 31 August 2027. Audit cases have until 31 October 2027.

    I missed the ITR deadline for FY 2025-26. What now?

    You can still file a belated return until 31 December 2026, with a late fee of ₹5,000 (₹1,000 if your income is up to ₹5 lakh). You may also lose some benefits, like carrying forward certain losses.

    What is the last date for tax-saving investments?

    31 March 2027 for FY 2026-27. Investments made after that count for the next year. These deductions only help in the old tax regime.

    Will I get reminders?

    Yes, if you add the deadlines with the Apple / Outlook button or "Add all": each event has an alert 3 days before. For Google Calendar, your usual default reminder applies.
