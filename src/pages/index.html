    Questions people ask

    Are the calculators really free?

    Yes. Every calculator, receipt and document tool is free with no sign-up. Only some premium biodata (₹20) and invitation (₹29) designs are paid; there are free designs too.

    Do you store my salary or personal details?

    No. Calculations run in your browser and are never sent to us. For biodata and invitation downloads we keep only your email and, for paid designs, the payment record.

    Which tax year do the calculators use?

    FY 2026-27 (assessment year 2027-28) by default, with FY 2025-26 available in the income tax calculator. We update the rules after every Union Budget.

    Can I put a calculator on my own website?

    Yes. Open any calculator, scroll to “Add this calculator to your website” and copy the code. It stays up to date automatically.
