How to use your UPI QR code

  1. Find your UPI ID in your UPI app (usually under your profile, like name@okhdfcbank or 98xxxxxx@ybl).
  2. Enter it above, add your shop name, and pick a design.
  3. Test it: scan the QR with another phone and check your name appears.
  4. Print it, laminate it, and put it where customers can easily scan it.

Personal or business UPI ID?

A personal UPI ID works for small shops, but banks may limit how many payments it can receive. For regular business, ask your bank or a business app for a merchant UPI ID: you'll get payment alerts, settlement reports and often a sound box.

Stay safe

  • Customers scan to pay. You never need to scan anything or enter your PIN to receive money.
  • Check your bank SMS or app before handing over goods. Fake "payment successful" screenshots are a common trick.
  • Check your standee now and then: fraudsters sometimes paste their own QR over a shop's.

Frequently asked questions

Which apps can scan this QR?

Any UPI app: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay, WhatsApp Pay and all bank UPI apps.

Is there any fee?

No. Making the QR here is free, and UPI payments from customers' bank accounts to you are free too.

Is my UPI ID stored?

No. The QR code is made in your browser. Your details are only saved on your own device for next time.
