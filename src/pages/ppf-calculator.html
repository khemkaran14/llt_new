How PPF interest is calculated

Interest is worked out on the lowest balance between the 5th and the last day of each month, and added once a year on 31 March. That's why depositing before 5 April earns you a full year's interest on that money.

Example: ₹1.5 lakh a year for 15 years at 7.1% grows to about ₹40.68 lakh. You deposit ₹22.5 lakh and earn about ₹18.18 lakh in tax-free interest.

Frequently asked questions

What is the PPF interest rate now?

7.1% a year for October–December 2026. The government reviews it every quarter, and it has stayed at 7.1% since April 2020.

Is PPF better than an FD?

For long-term saving, usually yes. PPF interest is completely tax-free, while FD interest is taxed at your slab rate. A 7.1% tax-free return is worth more than a 9% taxable FD for someone in the 30% bracket. The trade-off is the 15-year lock-in.

Can I get the 80C benefit in the new regime?

No. 80C deductions only apply in the old regime. PPF interest and maturity stay tax-free in both regimes, though.

What happens after 15 years?

You can withdraw everything, or extend the account in blocks of 5 years, with or without new deposits. Apply for extension within a year of maturity.
