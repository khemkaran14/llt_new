Why use these instead of a template?

  • No maths mistakes: GST, totals, round-off and amount in words are calculated for you.
  • Faster every time: your name, business and address are remembered on your device, and "Start next" moves the number up for you.
  • Get paid faster: invoices include a UPI QR code with the amount already filled in.
  • Private: everything happens in your browser. We never see your customers or amounts.

What we don't make, and why

We don't offer generators for fuel bills, restaurant bills, medical bills, hotel or travel bills, or salary slips. Those documents must come from the petrol pump, restaurant, hospital, hotel or employer. A self-made version used to claim reimbursement or a loan is fraud. Every generator here is for a document the person receiving the money issues, which is how receipts are meant to work.
