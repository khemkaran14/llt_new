What to include in a marriage biodata

  • Personal: name, date of birth, height, religion and community, and marital status.
  • Horoscope (if your family uses it): time and place of birth, rashi, nakshatra, gotra and manglik status.
  • Education and career: highest degree, job and where you work.
  • Family: parents' names and occupations, siblings and native place.
  • Contact: a family member's phone number and your city.
  • A recent photo: clear, well-lit and smiling.

Tips for a great biodata

  • Keep it to one page. Our designs adjust the text size to fit.
  • Be honest. Small details like height and income should match what you'll say in person.
  • Use a short, warm "About me" of 2–3 lines instead of a long essay.
  • Share the JPG on WhatsApp and keep the PDF for printing.
