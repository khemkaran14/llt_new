Why is in-hand salary less than CTC?

CTC (Cost to Company) is everything your employer spends on you in a year, not what you receive. These parts come out before your salary reaches you:

  • Employer's PF: 12% of basic (often capped at ₹1,800 a month) goes to your PF account. It's your money, but you can't spend it now.
  • Gratuity: about 4.81% of basic, paid only if you stay 5+ years.
  • Your own PF: another 12% of basic is deducted from your salary.
  • Professional tax: up to ₹2,500 a year, depending on your state.
  • Income tax (TDS): your yearly tax, spread over 12 months.
  • Variable pay: bonuses are part of CTC but are paid once or twice a year.

How to increase your take-home pay

  • Ask for employer NPS (80CCD(2)). Up to 14% of basic is tax-free even in the new regime.
  • Check which regime is cheaper. This calculator picks the lower one automatically.
  • Use the capped PF option if your employer offers it, to get more cash each month. Your retirement savings will grow more slowly.
  • Claim HRA in the old regime if you pay high rent in a big city.

Frequently asked questions

What is the in-hand salary for ₹12 lakh CTC?

About ₹93,800 a month under the new regime, with basic at 50% of CTC, PF capped at ₹1,800 a month and gratuity included in CTC. There's no income tax at this level in FY 2026-27, because taxable income stays under ₹12 lakh after the ₹75,000 standard deduction.

Is employer PF part of my CTC?

Usually yes. Most companies include their 12% PF share in CTC, which is why your gross salary is lower than CTC. Check your offer letter's salary structure.

What is the difference between gross salary and in-hand salary?

Gross salary is CTC minus employer PF and gratuity. In-hand salary is gross salary minus your own PF, professional tax and income tax.

Why does my in-hand pay change in some months?

Employers adjust TDS during the year, for example after you submit investment proofs in January–March, or when a bonus is paid. Your total tax for the year stays the same.
