How Indian wedding budgets usually split

Food and venue together usually take 40–50% of the budget. Outfits and jewellery are the next biggest item. Keep 5–10% as a buffer: almost every wedding goes over somewhere.

Ways to save without it showing

  • Cut the guest list first. Catering is charged per plate, so every 50 guests can save lakhs.
  • Pick an off-season date or a weekday: venues and decorators charge much less.
  • Combine functions, like haldi and mehendi on the same day at the same venue.
  • Send digital invitations on WhatsApp instead of printed boxes. Try our invitation maker.
  • Get 3 quotes for every big vendor, and pay advances by UPI or bank transfer so you have records.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an average Indian wedding cost?

It varies hugely by city and guest count: from a few lakh for a small ceremony to several crore. Mid-sized weddings with 200–400 guests in big cities commonly cost ₹10–30 lakh.

Is my budget saved?

Yes, in your own browser on this device. Use "Download Excel" to keep a copy or share it with family.
